Privacy Policy — Truth or Sip

Last updated: August 2026

Truth or Sip is a local "pass the phone" party game for adults. This policy explains what data it handles — which is essentially nothing.

Data we collect

Truth or Sip does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. No account, no sign-in, no internet connection required. Player names and game progress live only on your device during a session and are discarded when the game ends.

Permissions

The app uses your device's vibration for gameplay feedback. It does not access contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage, or any other sensitive feature.

Age

Truth or Sip is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It contains references to alcohol within the context of a social party game. The app collects no data from any user, including anyone under 18.

Third parties

No third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or trackers. If this changes in a future version, this policy and the Play Store data-safety section will be updated before release.

Contact

Questions: contact the developer via the app's Google Play listing page.
