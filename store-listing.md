## Full description
TRUTH OR SIP is the adult party game your group chat has been waiting for. One phone, your circle of friends, and questions that get the whole room talking.

HOW IT WORKS
Pass the phone around the circle. Each player gets a truth question — answer it honestly, or take a sip instead. Not drinking? Flip on Dares mode and play completely dry. Simple, fast, and endlessly replayable.

MADE FOR EVERY SHEREHE

• Fully offline — no accounts, no sign-ups, no data collected

PLAY RESPONSIBLY
Truth or Sip is about sips, not shots. Pace yourselves, drink water, and never pressure anyone. Dares mode keeps it 100% dry whenever you want.
